# Hiring Manager prep guide: your hiring strategy session

One page. Read it the day before the session with your recruiter.

## What the session is for

Your recruiter will run a one-hour meeting to turn your business need into a search plan.
You leave with an agreed candidate profile, a posting plan, an interview panel and
timeline, and a short list of Skills for Success that goes on the Workday requisition and
guides every screen and interview. The better your inputs, the faster the search.

## Bring answers to these

- Why the role is open, and what the last person brought that you will miss.
- The three to five skills you would not hire without, and what "good" looks like for each.
- What success at year one looks like, in a sentence.
- Why a strong candidate should want this job.
- Companies or people you already have in mind, and whether you will share the posting on
  your own networks.
- Your interview panel, alternates, and any dates to avoid.
- Your target start date.

## Skills for Success in 60 seconds

Workday now pre-fills each requisition with the skills tied to the job profile, usually
around nineteen. For Grade 20 and above roles, your recruiter will share the list on screen
and you will narrow it together to the 3 to 10 most critical to success, removing what is
broad or duplicated and adding anything missing. Come knowing which three you would not
hire without. In this phase the skills are not shown to candidates and do not change how
interviews run; they make sure the req describes the role accurately.

## Connection to Sport in 60 seconds

This is about athlete mindset: how someone prepares, competes, adapts, holds themselves
accountable, and works with others. It is not about playing sports, being a fan, or sport
knowledge. Be ready to say which two or three of those behaviors matter most in this role.

## If internal candidates apply

For Global Corporate Grade 20+ and Americas Supply Chain Grade 20+ roles, you will deliver
verbal feedback to internal candidates who complete the Business Interview, ideally within
3 to 5 business days. Workday will send you a task with instructions. Manager Center and
the Coaching through Feedback training can help.

## After the session

Expect a recap email within a day with decisions, the skills list, the panel and dates, and
open items with owners. The first talent brief is usually one to two weeks later.
