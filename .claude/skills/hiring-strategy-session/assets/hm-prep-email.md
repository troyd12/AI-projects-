# Strategy Pre-Meeting email to the Hiring Manager

Send 24 to 48 hours before the session. Keep it short; the questions do the work.

---

Subject: Prep for our hiring strategy session: [Job Title] ([date, time])

Hi [HM first name],

Thanks for making time on [day] for the strategy session on the [Job Title] role. The goal
of the meeting is to leave with a clear, shared search plan so we can post and start
sourcing right away.

To make the hour count, could you jot down short answers to these before we meet? Bullet
points are fine.

1. Why is this role open? If it is a backfill, what did the last person bring that the team
   will miss most?
2. What are the three to five skills you would not hire without?
3. What does success look like at the end of year one?
4. What is most appealing about this role to a strong candidate: the work, the team, the
   challenge, the path?
5. Which companies or teams do great work in this space, and is there anyone you already
   have in mind?
6. Who needs to be on the interview panel, and are there any out-of-office constraints in
   the next six to eight weeks?
7. When would you like this person to start?

One thing to think about ahead of time: as part of the Workday skills implementation, we
will agree on a short list of Skills for Success (3 to 10) that goes on the requisition and
guides screening and interviews. I will bring a draft list for you to react to, so no need
to build one from scratch.

We will also cover Connection to Sport. Quick note: this is about athlete mindset behaviors
(preparation, resilience, accountability, teamwork, adaptability), not whether someone
played sports.

Agenda and a shared notes doc are in the calendar invite. See you [day].

[Recruiter name]
