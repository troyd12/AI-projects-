# Hiring Strategy Session Guide (distilled)

Source: Nike, Inc. Global Corporate TA, "Hiring Strategy Session Guide". This is the baseline
every recruiter follows. It can be customized for HM familiarity, role maturity, and urgency,
but all core elements must be covered so that every search starts with the same quality and
shared understanding globally.

**What it is:** a recruiter-facilitated meeting (intake or kickoff) to gather critical
information and align with the Hiring Manager on the hiring strategy before the search begins.

Contents
1. Before the session: Laying the Groundwork
2. During the session: Translate Business Need into Talent Strategy (sections A to G)
3. After the session: Activate the Search

---

## 1. Before the session: Laying the Groundwork

### Session preparation checklist (recruiter)

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| Prepare the HM for the session | Send the Strategy Pre-Meeting email and ask the HM to complete the prep questions at least 24 to 48 hours before the session. |
| Assemble the job description | Collect, review, and prepare the JD for posting, including MYNEXT verbiage where applicable. |
| Check requisition details in Workday | Job title, req / position numbers, grade, location, approvals, project categories, posting requirements. |
| Review compensation inputs | HRBP / TA Leader guidance from Proactive Teaming, Workday position details, peer data in the Offer Modeling Tool (salary range, target salary, internal peer data in the same location, percentiles). Consult TRC if peer data is not in the Offer Modeling Tool. |
| Schedule the session | Put the agenda in the calendar invite, link a shared note-taking doc (CoPilot page, Box Note, etc.), and allow enough time for every question the HM might have. |

### Strategy Pre-Meeting Form (completed by recruiter)

Left column: Job Title, Req Number, Hiring Manager, Job Code, Global Grade, Salary Range,
Target Salary (budget), Direct reports? , Non-compete required? (check the position), Posting
Strategy (internal or internal/external), Posting Duration (minimum one calendar week).

Right column: Drug screen required? (Y/N), Capability to sponsor work visa if needed?, Job
Location (city, state) and additional approved locations, Work Location Arrangements (remote,
etc.), Relocation provided? (Y/N), Referral bonus eligible and amount, Opportunity to consider
retail talent via MYNEXT promotion?

## 2. During the session: Translate Business Need into Talent Strategy

Recruiter-facilitated. Use the prompts to guide the conversation, capture decisions, and
define next steps. Bring to the meeting:

- Sourcing approach: target companies and markets, internal talent pools, any known prospects.
  Promote applicable roles to retail teammates using MYNEXT in the JD.
- Proposed interview panel, alternates / proxies, scheduling constraints; confirm admin or
  studio support if applicable.
- Links and resources: Manager Center guidance and the Coaching through Feedback training for
  internal candidate feedback.

### Recruiter skills guidance (sidebar)

- Align on a focused set of 3 to 10 Optional Skills that best represent success in the role
  (Corporate and Firstline+ Leadership roles).
- Prioritize critical skills needed for success over comprehensive wish lists.
- Use skills as one input to hiring decisions alongside experience, Leadership Defined,
  Connection to Sport, and demonstrated performance.

### A. Team and Role Insight

- Why is this role open: new or backfill? If backfill, why is the incumbent leaving, and what
  key skills did they bring to the team?
- What are the skills you must have for this role?
- Given the skills required, would a retail athlete's consumer expertise, brand passion, and
  operational experience translate to this role? Should we actively promote it through MYNEXT?
- What would the potential career path for someone in this role look like?
- What does success look like?
- To define the target candidate profile, identify and align on the skills needed to succeed.
  These skills go on the Workday job requisition to create clarity and consistency through
  the hiring process. (Grade 20+: share the req on screen, review the defaulted Optional
  Skills and Suggested Skills, prioritize 3 to 10. See `skills-on-reqs-program.md`.)
- How can this position best be sold to a candidate? What is most appealing: the role, the
  challenge, the business team?
- How would you describe your team dynamics? Skill gaps?

### B. Connection to Sport Assessment

See `connection-to-sport.md` for the introduction, HM talking point, HM questions, and
candidate questions.

### C. Posting Strategy (including ACE questions)

- Confirm posting strategy (internal only vs internal/external, and promoting to retail
  teammates via MYNEXT), posting duration, and compliance needs (drug screen, non-compete).
- Capture any ACE framework questions or inputs used by the organization.

### D. Search Strategy and Plan

- (External search) What companies are doing great work in this space? Have we hired anyone
  from there in the past?
- Are you aware of any candidates who may be interested in this role?
- Are you willing to activate your broader social networks (LinkedIn, etc.) and professional
  associations to share this opportunity?

### E. Interview Process and Timing

- Who will be on your interviews? Required interviewers. Alternates / proxies.
- Is there an admin or studio manager to help with the interview process and ensure a top
  candidate experience?
- When is your team available to begin interviews? Any out-of-office constraints (PTO,
  sabbatical, conferences)?
- Desired start date for the candidate?

### F. Wrap Up: High-Level Plan from Recruiter

Overview of how Workday will be used in the recruiting process: resume sharing, interview
scheduling, interview feedback. Agree the check-in / search update cadence: weekly or
bi-weekly.

### G. Interview Reminders (Internal Candidate Feedback)

- Review the internal feedback process with the HM.
- HMs must deliver verbal feedback to internal candidates (Global Corp Grade 20+ and Americas
  Supply Chain 20+) who complete the Business Interview step, preferably within 3 to 5
  business days.
- Remind the HM they will receive a Workday task with instructions for the internal candidate
  feedback delivery process.
- Resources: Manager Center; Coaching through Feedback training.

## 3. After the session: Activate the Search

Post-strategy meeting checklist (recruiter):

1. Follow up with a thank-you email including key recaps and links to the LinkedIn posting.
2. Schedule the first talent brief (TB) for 1 to 2 weeks after the strategy meeting.
3. Document the agreed search strategy, interview panel, and timelines in the recruiting plan.
4. Confirm open or action items (comp, leveling, posting, assessments) and owners with due dates.
5. Confirm the agreed Skills for Success are accurately reflected on the Workday job
   requisition before posting (Optional Skills field only, Required Skills empty, no changes
   after posting).
