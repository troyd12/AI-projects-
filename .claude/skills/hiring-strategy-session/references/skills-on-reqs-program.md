# Skills on Job Requisitions: the program (TA Readiness, FY27)

Source: TA Enablement deck "Skills on Job Requisitions", Corporate and Firstline+, FY27.
Read this before advising anyone on what to do in Workday. It is the operating reality the
strategy session guide's "Skills for Success" checklist item sits inside.

## What skills are (level set)

Skills are the abilities you use to get work done: practical, learnable, built through
experience and education. Two categories on job profiles:

- Technical skills (job-specific): what you need to do the work of a role. Examples: data
  analysis, vendor negotiation.
- Interpersonal skills (how you work with others): communication, collaboration.

Teammates own the skills on their Talent Profile and Talent Card. Job reqs describe what a
role needs. The library is 200,000+ skills (Lightcast), and learning content is tagged to it.

## What was built

- Lightcast drafted 15 to 20 skills per job profile from market data.
- TA Peer Coaches and Talent COEs reviewed every job family; Business HR validated 638
  priority Sport Offense and Firstline roles.
- Batch A is live in Workday: about 2,000 active job profiles, roughly 19 skills per profile
  on average, 13,500+ skills reviewed. These are the skills that auto-populate on reqs.
- Batch B (about 1,500 more profiles) is mapped but not validated or loaded. Reqs on those
  profiles get no auto-populated skills; Workday still shows Suggested Skills.

Skills on job reqs launched October 22. Workday work instructions in the TA Playbook from
October 28; Firstline+ grade-specific resources from November 2.

## The three Workday fields

| Field | What it is | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Optional Skills | Skills relevant to successful performance. Validated Job Profile skills auto-populate here and are the starting point for alignment. | The only approved field. Edit here. |
| Suggested Skills | System-generated recommendations. | Reference to help ideate; never a substitute for HM alignment. |
| Required Skills | Not approved for this process. | Never use. Workday validation blocks the req from progressing if anything is entered. |

Per Nike Legal guidance, Optional Skills is the only field approved for entering skills on
job requisitions. Treat this as a hard rule, not a preference.

## Workflow by grade

**Grade 20+ (recruiter-led alignment)**
1. Recruiter is assigned to the open role.
2. Recruiter opens the requisition and reviews the defaulted Optional Skills before the
   strategy session.
3. Strategy session: recruiter shares screen, reviews defaulted Optional Skills and Suggested
   Skills with the HM, and they prioritize 3 to 10 skills most relevant to role success.
4. Recruiter edits the req: updates Optional Skills to the aligned list, confirms no Required
   Skills are entered.
5. Recruiter posts. Skills are visible to the HM once the req is opened, not to candidates.
   Do not change the aligned skills after posting.

**Grade 10 and 15 (system-led population)**
- Validated Job Profile skills auto-populate and should remain unchanged. No alignment, no
  edits. Skills are not visible to the HM during req creation. Recruiter posts as usual.

## Where the program is (Crawl, Walk, Run)

| Phase | Timing | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Crawl: enable skills in reqs | FY27 Q2 (now) | Skills on reqs in Workday. Recruiter and HM align for Grade 20+. No change to how candidates are assessed. |
| Walk: activate skills across hiring | ~FY27 Q3 to Q4 | Skills visible to candidates on postings. Integrated into interviews and evaluations. Recruiter, HM, and interviewer readiness expands. |
| Run: matching and intelligence | ~FY27 Q4 to FY28 | Skills-based hiring at scale. Personalized talent and job recommendations. Connected enterprise tools. |

### What this is, in Crawl
- A foundational step toward a consistent, enterprise-wide approach to skills.
- Auto-population of reqs with validated Job Profile skills.
- An opportunity for recruiters and HMs to align on the skills most relevant to role
  success during the strategy session (Grade 20+ only).
- A recruiter-owned process for updating Optional Skills based on that alignment.

### What this is not, in Crawl
- Not candidate-facing.
- Not standalone criteria for assessing candidates.
- Not integrated into interview guides or Workday candidate feedback tasks.

So in Crawl the value is role clarity, consistency, better data, and future talent access,
not a new screening or interview tool. Say this plainly to HMs who expect the list to drive
their interviews; it will later (Walk), not yet.

## Leading the alignment in the strategy session (Grade 20+)

**01 Frame the purpose.** Introduce it as defining what success looks like in the role,
not as an administrative Workday task.
Suggested language: "As part of our strategy session, we'll review the skills associated
with this role and align on those most critical to success. This helps ensure the
requisition accurately reflects the capabilities needed."

**02 Evaluate the defaulted skills.** Review the validated Job Profile skills together, as
the starting point. Guiding questions:
- Which skills are most directly connected to the outcomes we discussed?
- Which skills are too broad, duplicative, or less relevant?
- Are any critical skills missing?

**03 Prioritize and confirm.** Align on a focused 3 to 10.
Suggested closing: "We've aligned on the skills most critical to this role. I'll update
the requisition to reflect our discussion, and you'll be able to review those skills in
Workday once the requisition is posted."

## Scenarios

**Too many skills.** "A longer list can make it harder to distinguish the capabilities
that matter most. Let's focus on the skills most directly connected to success in this role."

**Too few skills.** "Let's revisit the key responsibilities and outcomes for this role to
determine whether there are additional skills that are important to successful performance."

**No auto-populated skills (Batch B profile).** Same alignment process. Partner with the HM
on the 3 to 10, search for each in the Optional Skills field, add them manually before
posting. Suggested Skills can help ideate but do not replace HM alignment.

**Defaulted skills do not fit the role.** Review which do not align with responsibilities
or outcomes, discuss with the HM, remove and add in Optional Skills, confirm the final 3 to
10 before posting, and flag the profile-validation concern to your designated Leader or
Peer Coach so the Job Profile gets fixed for the next req.
