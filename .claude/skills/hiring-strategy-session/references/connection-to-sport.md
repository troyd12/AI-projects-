# Connection to Sport

Connection to Sport is part of how Nike assesses talent. It focuses on **athlete mindset
behaviors and sport fluency**: competitive drive, preparation, resilience, accountability,
teamwork, adaptability, curiosity. It is not about whether a candidate played sports, is a
fan, or has deep sport knowledge. It is about behaviors, not participation.

The most common failure mode is an HM or interviewer treating it as a sports-biography
question. When that happens, candidates with the strongest athlete mindset but no playing
history get marked down, and the panel's data is noise. Set this expectation up front.

## HM talking point (say this before getting into role specifics)

"Before we get into role specifics, I want to align on Connection to Sport. It's a core part
of how we assess talent. We're looking for evidence of an athlete mindset: how someone
prepares, competes, adapts, and works with others, and how that shows up in their work.
There's no one 'right' background; it's about behaviors, not participation."

## HM-facing wording (for prep guides and emails)

"Connection to Sport is part of how Nike assesses every candidate. It's about athlete
mindset: how someone prepares, competes, adapts under pressure, holds themselves accountable,
and works with a team. It is not about whether they played or follow sports. Come ready to
say which two or three of those behaviors matter most in this role."

## Questions to ask the Hiring Manager

- What does strong Connection to Sport look like in this role?
- How directly does this role influence athletes, sport culture, consumer experience, or
  sport storytelling?
- Which athlete mindset behaviors are most critical for success in this role?
- How important is sport fluency vs athlete mindset for this position?

Capture the answers as two or three named behaviors (for example, "preparation and
resilience under launch pressure") so the interview panel can probe for the same things.

## Questions to ask the candidate

- What does the Power of Sport mean to you? What is your personal connection?
- What does having an athlete mindset mean to you, and how do you translate that mindset
  into your daily work and drive for winning results?
- Optional, for sport-led or priority roles: Tell me about your personal connection to
  [blank] sport. How has the game shown up in your life: playing, competing, coaching,
  following, or being part of [blank] sport culture?

## Calibrating by role

| Role proximity to sport | Weight on sport fluency | Weight on athlete mindset |
|---|---|---|
| Sport-led or athlete-facing (product, athlete services, sport marketing) | High | High |
| Consumer-facing but not sport-specific (retail experience, events) | Medium | High |
| Enterprise or back-office (architecture, supply chain, finance) | Low | High |

The athlete mindset column is always high. That is the point of the assessment.
