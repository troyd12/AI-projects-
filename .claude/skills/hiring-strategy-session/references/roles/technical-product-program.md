# Role library: Technical Product Manager and Technical Program Manager

Covers technical product managers, platform product managers, and technical program
managers in Global Technology.

## Likely Workday skill labels

Look for these on the defaulted list or search them in the Optional Skills field.

- Product Management / Technical Product Management
- Program Management / Project Management
- Product Roadmapping
- Requirements Gathering / Requirements Analysis
- Stakeholder Management
- Agile Methodologies
- Data Analysis
- Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Risk Management
- Communication

## What usually makes these searches hard

- Product vs program. Product owns the what and why; program owns the how and when. HMs
  often want both; section A has to say which one the role is judged on.
- "Technical" means different things. Ask whether the person must read code, design APIs,
  or simply hold their own with engineers. Pick labels accordingly.
- Platform roles with internal customers need stakeholder skills more than market skills.

## What the labels mean for this role (recruiter notes for keep / remove / add)

1. Product Management or Program Management (pick the one the role is judged on): owns a
   roadmap or a delivery plan end to end; evidence is a launch they drove and its outcome.
2. Product Roadmapping: sequences work against capacity and dependencies; evidence is a
   roadmap that changed when constraints changed.
3. Requirements Analysis: turns ambiguous asks into specs engineers can build; evidence is
   a spec they wrote and what engineers said about it.
4. Stakeholder Management: aligns engineering, business, and leadership; evidence is a
   disagreement they resolved.
5. Data Analysis: makes decisions from usage and performance data; evidence is a decision
   the data changed.
6. Risk Management (programs): sees the slip coming and acts; evidence is a program they
   pulled back on track.

Often requested, usually learnable or belongs in notes: a specific tool (Jira, Aha), Nike
platform knowledge, domain certification.

## Sharpest section A questions for this family

- Is this role accountable for outcomes (product) or delivery (program), and which metric
  proves it?
- Who are the customers, internal or external, and who signs off on priorities?
- How technical does this person need to be, concretely: read code, design APIs, or neither?
- What program or product went sideways recently, and what would this hire have done?

## Connection to Sport angle

Low to medium sport fluency (consumer-facing products raise it), high athlete mindset.
Probe for adaptability (a pivot they led), accountability, and competitive drive.

## Sourcing targets (section D)

- Product and program organizations at consumer, commerce, and platform companies.
- Internal: engineers and analysts moving toward product, program managers in adjacent orgs.
- Professional product and program communities; the HM's network.
