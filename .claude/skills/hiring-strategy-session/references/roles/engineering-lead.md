# Role library: Engineering Lead (manager, director, staff-plus)

Covers software engineering managers, senior managers and directors, and staff or principal
engineers who lead through others. Typical context at Nike: Global Technology platforms,
commerce, data, enterprise applications, workplace technology.

## Likely Workday skill labels

Look for these on the defaulted list or search them in the Optional Skills field.

- Software Development / Software Engineering
- Engineering Management / People Management
- Agile Methodologies
- System Design / Software Architecture
- Technical Leadership
- Stakeholder Management
- Cloud Computing (name the platform in your notes)
- DevOps / Continuous Delivery
- Performance Management / Coaching
- Communication

## What usually makes these searches hard

- "Hands-on" is the question. Ask how many hours a week the person writes or reviews code,
  and whether that changes in year two. The answer decides whether Software Development is
  a top-three skill or a supporting one.
- Platform depth vs leadership breadth. A director over four teams needs different labels
  from a manager of one. Section A has to name team count, scope, and who they influence.
- Internal candidates are common and strong. Section G applies for Grade 20+.

## What the labels mean for this role (recruiter notes for keep / remove / add)

1. Technical Leadership: sets direction and standards for engineering teams; evidence is a
   practice or architecture that other teams adopted.
2. Engineering Management: hires, grows, and holds a team to delivery; evidence is a team
   they built and what changed under them.
3. System Design: can judge and shape designs the team proposes; evidence is a design
   review they ran and the trade-off they forced.
4. Agile Methodologies: runs a delivery cadence that ships; evidence is cycle time or
   release frequency they moved.
5. Stakeholder Management: translates product and business asks into engineering plans and
   back; evidence is a conflict they resolved between product and engineering.
6. Cloud Computing / DevOps: owns the operational health of what the team runs; evidence is
   an incident and what they changed afterward.

Often requested, usually learnable or belongs in notes: a specific language or framework,
Nike platform knowledge, a specific cloud vendor certification.

## Sharpest section A questions for this family

- How many engineers, how many teams, and does this role write code? How many hours a week?
- What does the team ship, and what is the metric the leader is judged on?
- What has the team struggled with that this hire must fix in the first six months?
- Who does this leader negotiate with most: product, architecture, another engineering
  org, or vendors?

## Connection to Sport angle

Low sport fluency, high athlete mindset. Probe for accountability (an outage or missed
release they owned), teamwork (how they lead a crew through a hard quarter), and preparation.

## Sourcing targets (section D)

- Engineering orgs at consumer and commerce companies with similar scale and platform mix.
- Internal: senior and staff engineers ready to lead, managers in adjacent domains.
- The HM's own network; engineering leaders know who they would hire again.
