# DocuSign – compensation & benefits (public sources)

Researched 09/02/2026 for req R-90323 (Principal Engineer, Identity & Authorization).
Context: candidate is a current DocuSign engineer in the Seattle area who declined to
share his current salary.

## Does DocuSign publish this?

**Partly.** There is no published DocuSign compensation philosophy document. Two things
*are* officially published:

1. **Salary ranges in job postings.** Washington's pay transparency law requires
   employers to post a salary range and a general benefits description in every WA job
   ad. DocuSign complies. This is the most reliable public number available — pull a live
   DocuSign posting for a comparable Seattle/Bellevue engineering role and read the band
   directly.
2. **Executive compensation** in the annual DEF 14A proxy (DocuSign is public, NASDAQ:
   DOCU). Not useful at this level.

Everything else below is crowd-sourced from levels.fyi, Glassdoor and Comparably —
directional, self-reported, and unaudited. Treat as a range, not a quote.

## Equity

DocuSign is publicly traded, so **RSUs are standard, not options**.

- Vesting: 4 years — 25% at year one, then 6.25% quarterly.
- **ESPP:** contribute up to 15% of base salary, 15% discount on purchase price.

This matters for the screen: the candidate asked about *total* comp, which suggests he
is carrying unvested RSUs. Any offer needs to account for what he walks away from.

## Benefits

- **401(k):** 50% match on the first 6% of base.
- **PTO:** 20 days.
- **Parental leave:** up to 6 months paid (birth, adoption, foster placement).
- **Health:** 100% employer-paid plan options from day one; 100% mental health coverage;
  $30K family-forming benefit.
- **Other:** custom workstation, $80/month phone reimbursement, adoption and fertility
  assistance.
- Glassdoor benefits rating 4.4/5 (672 reviews); aggregate benefits value estimated at
  ~$12,786/employee.
