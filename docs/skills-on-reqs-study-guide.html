Skills on Reqs

What the Skills on Job Requisitions launch means, what you do in Workday step by step, and where the skills library lives. Written for a corporate recruiter who has fifteen minutes.

LESSON 1

Start here: the whole thing in five sentences

Workday, Nike's applicant tracking system, now pre-fills a list of about 19 skills on every new job requisition, pulled from the role's validated Job Profile. For Grade 20 and above roles, you review that list with the hiring manager during the strategy session and narrow it to the 3 to 10 skills most critical to success. You make the edits in one field only, Optional Skills, and never touch Required Skills. You finish before posting and do not change the list afterward. Right now candidates never see the skills and interviewers do not score against them; this phase is about getting the requisition right.

The one habit to build: open the req and look at the defaulted Optional Skills before the strategy session, so the conversation starts from the list and not from a blank page.
LESSON 2

Words to know

Skill
An ability you use to get work done. Practical, learnable, built over time. Two kinds on job profiles: technical (data analysis, vendor negotiation) and interpersonal (communication, collaboration).
Job Profile
The Workday record that defines a job. Each validated profile now carries 15 to 20 skills. A req inherits the skills of its Job Profile.
Optional Skills
The Workday field on the requisition where the profile's skills land and where you edit. The only field approved for skills, per Nike Legal.
Suggested Skills
Skills Workday recommends on its own. Use them to spark ideas. They never replace hiring manager alignment.
Required Skills
A field you never use. If anything is entered, Workday validation stops the req from moving forward.
Skills Cloud
Workday's skills library of 200,000+ entries. You reach it by typing in the Optional Skills field; it is the search behind that box.
Skills alignment
The Grade 20+ conversation where recruiter and hiring manager agree on the 3 to 10 skills most relevant to success. It happens inside the strategy session.
Batch A / Batch B
Batch A: about 2,000 profiles already validated and live, so their reqs auto-fill. Batch B: about 1,500 more, mapped but not loaded, so their reqs start empty.
Talent Profile / Talent Card
Where teammates list their own skills. Teammates own that side; reqs describe what a role needs.
Crawl, Walk, Run
The three phases of the rollout. We are in Crawl: skills on reqs, no change to candidate assessment.
LESSON 3

Why Nike is doing this

Skills give recruiters, managers, and teammates one way to describe work. For the business that means clearer hiring criteria, better visibility into which skills already exist inside Nike (build, buy, or borrow), and cleaner data for workforce planning and internal mobility. For teammates it means a visible Talent Profile that roles and projects can find, and development goals tied to a library of learning.

For you, the payoff in this phase is role clarity: you and the hiring manager agree on what matters before anything is posted.

LESSON 5

Does this apply to my req?

Grade decides everything. Check the global grade on the requisition first.

  • Grade 20 and above: you lead skills alignment with the hiring manager and edit Optional Skills.
  • Grade 10 and 15: nothing to do. The validated skills populate automatically and stay unchanged. The hiring manager does not see them during req creation.
Firstline+ grade-specific guidance arrives November 2. Until then, follow the grade rule above.
LESSON 6

Step by step in Workday (Grade 20+)

Six steps from assignment to posting. The screens are simplified sketches of the requisition's Qualifications area; your exact clicks are in the Workday Work Instructions in the TA Playbook from October 28.

1

Open the requisition and read the defaulted skills

Before the strategy session, open the req in Workday and find the skills section. Optional Skills will already hold the Job Profile's validated skills, usually around 19. Read them against the job description and note which look broad, duplicated, or off.

Where: the job requisition, Qualifications area, Optional Skills field. If it is empty, the profile is in Batch B; see Lesson 9.

2

Share your screen in the strategy session

With the hiring manager, walk the Optional Skills list and glance at Suggested Skills. Frame it as defining success in the role, not as a Workday chore. Ask: which skills connect most directly to the outcomes we just discussed, which are too broad or duplicated, and what is missing?

Where: your screen, the same Qualifications area. Lesson 8 has the words to use.

3

Remove what does not fit

Take off skills the hiring manager says are not relevant, too broad, or repeat another entry. Removing is most of the work: nineteen rarely survive to ten. If the defaulted list is badly wrong for the role, finish the alignment anyway and then flag the profile to your Leader or Peer Coach so it is fixed for the next req.

Where: Edit Job Requisition, Optional Skills field, the remove control on each skill.

4

Add what is missing from the skills library

For any critical skill the hiring manager names that is not on the list, click into the Optional Skills field and start typing. The search that appears is the Workday Skills Cloud, the 200,000-entry library. Pick the closest validated entry. Prefer a specific skill over a broad one when both exist.

Where: the Optional Skills field itself. Typing opens the library; there is no separate menu to find. Lesson 7 shows it up close.

5

Confirm 3 to 10, and that Required Skills is empty

Count the list. Between 3 and 10, each one the hiring manager can explain in a sentence. Then check the Required Skills field: it must be empty. Anything in it blocks the req. Save, and read the final list back to the hiring manager so there is no surprise later.

Where: Edit Job Requisition, Qualifications area, both fields visible together.

6

Post, then leave the skills alone

Post the role as usual. The hiring manager can now see the aligned skills on the requisition in Workday. Candidates cannot; that comes in the Walk phase. Do not change the aligned skills after posting. If something must change, talk to your Leader or Peer Coach rather than editing.

Where: your normal posting flow. Put the final list in your recap email so it is on record.

LESSON 7

Where the skills library is

Recruiters ask "where do I go to get the skills?" The answer is that you do not go anywhere. The library, Workday's Skills Cloud, sits behind the Optional Skills field on the requisition. Click into the field, type a few letters, and the matching library entries appear. Choose one and it becomes a skill on the req.

  • Search by the words the hiring manager used. Try two or three phrasings if the first returns nothing useful.
  • Suggested Skills, shown near the same area, is Workday's own shortlist for this profile. Scan it for ideas, then still search and confirm with the hiring manager.
  • Only entries from the library can be added. If a skill truly does not exist, pick the nearest one and note the gap for your Peer Coach.
Three fields sit together on the req. Optional Skills is where you work. Suggested Skills is a hint list. Required Skills is off limits.

LESSON 8

Running the conversation

Three moves inside the strategy session, with the deck's suggested words. Applies to Grade 20+ only.

1

Frame the purpose

Position it as defining success, not admin.

"As part of our strategy session, we'll review the skills associated with this role and align on those most critical to success. This helps ensure the requisition accurately reflects the capabilities needed."
2

Evaluate the defaulted skills

The existing list is the starting point. Ask three questions.

  • Which skills are most directly connected to the outcomes we discussed?
  • Which skills are too broad, duplicative, or less relevant?
  • Are any critical skills missing?
3

Prioritize and confirm

Land on 3 to 10 and close the loop.

"We've aligned on the skills most critical to this role. I'll update the requisition to reflect our discussion, and you'll be able to review those skills in Workday once the requisition is posted."
LESSON 9

Four scenarios you will hit

Too many skills
Say: "A longer list can make it harder to distinguish the capabilities that matter most. Let's focus on the skills most directly connected to success in this role."
Too few skills
Say: "Let's revisit the key responsibilities and outcomes for this role to determine whether there are additional skills that are important to successful performance."
Nothing auto-populated
The profile is in Batch B. Same process: align on 3 to 10 with the HM, search each in the Optional Skills field, add manually before posting. Suggested Skills can help you ideate.
Defaults do not fit the role
Review which do not match the responsibilities, discuss with the HM, remove and add in Optional Skills, confirm 3 to 10, and flag the profile to your Leader or Peer Coach so it is corrected.
LESSON 10

Crawl, Walk, Run

What this is now and what it becomes. Knowing the phase keeps you from over-promising to hiring managers.

Crawl
FY27 Q2, live now
  • Skills on reqs in Workday
  • Recruiter and HM align for Grade 20+
  • No change to how candidates are assessed
  • Not candidate-facing, not in interview guides or feedback tasks
Walk
About FY27 Q3 to Q4
  • Skills visible to candidates on postings
  • Integrated into interviews and evaluations
  • Recruiter, HM, and interviewer readiness expands
Run
About FY27 Q4 to FY28
  • Skills-based hiring at scale
  • Personalized talent and job recommendations
  • Connected enterprise tools
If a hiring manager asks "so interviewers will score against these?", the answer today is no. Skills are not standalone assessment criteria in Crawl. They sharpen the req and build the data for Walk.
LESSON 11

Self-check

Answer in your head, then open each one.

A Grade 15 req opens with 18 skills on it. What do you do?

Nothing. Grade 10 and 15 reqs use the validated skills automatically and they stay unchanged. Post as usual.

The hiring manager wants 14 skills. What do you say?

"A longer list can make it harder to distinguish the capabilities that matter most. Let's focus on the skills most directly connected to success in this role." Then narrow to 3 to 10.

Where do you find the skills library?

Inside the Optional Skills field on the requisition. Click in, type, and the Skills Cloud matches appear. There is no separate screen to visit.

Which field do you never use, and why?

Required Skills. It is not approved per Nike Legal, and Workday validation blocks the req from progressing if anything is entered.

The req opened with no skills at all. Is the process different?

No. The profile is in Batch B. Align on 3 to 10 with the HM, search each in Optional Skills, add them manually before posting.

The HM wants to swap a skill a week after posting. Can you?

No. Do not change aligned skills after posting. Raise it with your Leader or Peer Coach.

Will candidates see the skills on the posting?

Not in Crawl. That arrives in the Walk phase, around FY27 Q3 to Q4.

The defaulted skills look like they belong to a different job. What do you do?

Align with the HM anyway (remove, add, confirm 3 to 10), then flag the Job Profile validation concern to your designated Leader or Peer Coach.
