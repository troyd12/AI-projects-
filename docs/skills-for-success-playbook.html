Skills for Success Playbook

How recruiters and hiring managers agree on the right skills for a Workday requisition, in nine plays. Built from the Global Corporate TA Hiring Strategy Session Guide.

For: Recruiters and Hiring ManagersWhen: Before, during, and after the strategy sessionOutput: 3 to 10 skills on the req before posting
PLAY 1

Three rules that never change

Everything else in this playbook is a way of meeting these three rules. If a skills list breaks one of them, it goes back to the hiring manager before it goes on the requisition.

3 to 10
Optional Skills on the req
A list of 15 is a wish list. Wish lists get cut.
Top 3
Critical skills first
The ones you would refuse to hire without lead the list.
1 of 5
Skills are one input
Alongside experience, Leadership Defined, Connection to Sport, and demonstrated performance.
PLAY 2

Where skills show up in the session

Skills are not a separate meeting. They thread through the strategy session's three stages, and each stage has one skills job.

1

Before

Recruiter sends the prep email 24 to 48 hours ahead and drafts 6 to 8 skills from the role library and the JD.

Skills job: bring a draft the HM can react to
2

During

Section A, Team and Role Insight, is where the HM names must-have skills. The recruiter runs cut, rank, add until the list is 3 to 10.

Skills job: agree the list and the critical three
3

After

Recap email carries the list. Recruiter confirms it is accurately reflected on the Workday requisition before posting.

Skills job: get it onto the req, then post
PLAY 3

Write skills as things people do

A skill is an observable capability, not a topic. Topics cannot be screened for or interviewed against. Capabilities can. The test: could an interviewer ask for an example of it?

  • Start with a verb: designs, negotiates, builds, presents, runs.
  • Name the scale or context: multi-region, to budget, three distribution centers.
  • Keep tools and systems out of the skill line. They belong in the evidence.

PLAY 4

Every skill carries its evidence

The evidence line is what makes the list usable by interviewers who were not in the strategy session. It tells the panel what to look for in a resume, a work sample, or an answer.

Write it as a question the interviewer could ask, or a thing the resume should show.

Skill statement
Plans and executes events end to end at the scale this role recurs at, on time and on budget.
Evidence the panel looks for
Three recent events with scope, budget, headcount, and outcome. Ask: what went wrong on the biggest one, and what did you do in the next ten minutes?
Critical or supporting? Critical. The HM would not hire without it.
PLAY 5

Cut, rank, add

Hiring managers build better lists by reacting than by inventing. Bring a draft and run three passes. Stop when the list is 3 to 10 and the HM can explain every item in one sentence.

  • Cut. Would you still hire a strong candidate who lacked this? Then remove it.
  • Rank. Which three would you refuse to hire without? Those are critical.
  • Add. What does your best performer do that is not on the list?

If the HM pushes past ten, ask which could be learned in the first 90 days. Those move to the onboarding plan, not the requisition.

PLAY 6

Critical skills first, then supporting, each with its evidence line. Anything learnable in 90 days goes to the onboarding plan and stays off the req. Confirm the exact taxonomy names in Workday before posting.

PLAY 7

Connection to Sport is a behavior, not a biography

It sits beside skills as its own input. Name the two or three athlete mindset behaviors that matter most for the role in the session, so the panel probes for the same things.

"We're looking for evidence of an athlete mindset: how someone prepares, competes, adapts, and works with others, and how that shows up in their work. There's no one right background. It's about behaviors, not participation."

It is

  • Competitive drive
  • Preparation
  • Resilience
  • Accountability
  • Teamwork
  • Adaptability
  • Curiosity

It is not

  • Whether they played sports
  • Being a fan
  • Deep sport knowledge
  • A sports biography question

Sport fluency matters more for sport-led roles. Athlete mindset matters for every role.

PLAY 8

Starter lists by role family

Drafts to react to, not answers. Each shows the three skills most often critical for that family. The HM cuts, ranks, and adds from here.

Principal Architect

  1. Designs end-to-end architectures across systems, teams, and regions
  2. Sets technical direction other teams adopt without direct authority
  3. Makes build, buy, or partner recommendations an executive can decide on
Watch for: wanting stack depth, enterprise breadth, and executive influence in one person. Usually Grade 20+, so internal feedback rules apply.

Event Planner

  1. Plans and executes events end to end at the scale that recurs, on time and on budget
  2. Manages venues, AV, catering, and production partners to the brief
  3. Manages budgets with forecasting, change control, and reconciliation
Watch for: sizing the list to the HM's most memorable event instead of the typical one. Retail talent via MYNEXT often fits.

Inventory and Supply Chain

  1. Builds and runs replenishment plans balancing service, working capital, and cost
  2. Forecasts demand and reconciles it with supply constraints
  3. Analyzes operational data to find root causes
Watch for: a planning system named as a critical skill. Treat it as evidence of a broader planning capability.
PLAY 9

Avoid these, then post

The mistakes on the left show up in most first drafts. The checklist on the right is the recruiter's gate before the requisition goes live.

×
Listing tools instead of capabilities. Tools go in the evidence line.
×
Copying JD responsibility bullets as skills. Responsibilities describe the job; skills describe the person who does it well.
×
Leaving influence and stakeholder skills off technical roles. Senior hires fail on influence more than on depth.
×
Writing the list for a competitor's ideal hire instead of for what success on this team looks like.
×
Treating the skills list as the whole scorecard.
